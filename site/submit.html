How apps get into the store

Like Apple's App Store, the W-OS App Store is curated. The W-OS team approves every app and every update, and can withdraw an app at any time.

  1. Send us your appEmail the details below to support@willgraves.co.uk. A submission form is coming; email is the process for now.
  2. We review itWe check it works on W-OS, does what its description says, and is safe for people to install.
  3. We list itApproved apps are added to the W-OS signed store list. Devices only trust that list, so nothing else can appear in the store.
  4. Updates go the same waySend a new version and we review and publish it. We can also withdraw an app if a problem is found.

What we won't list

  • Apps that collect data without saying so
  • Apps that ask for more access than they need, or try to reach outside their own folders
  • Apps that copy another app's name or branding
  • Web apps that are not served over https://
